About me

Detail-obsessed, design-minded, and made for the inbox.

I'm a WordPress & Front-End Developer with a passion for building clean, user-friendly experiences. My favorite work lives at the intersection of design and code — taking a written brief, graphic, or Figma mockup and turning it into a polished, on-brand result that works everywhere.

A big part of my day is hand-coding HTML and CSS and troubleshooting how it renders across devices and clients. I care deeply about the details: responsive layouts, cross-browser QA, accessibility (WCAG 2.1), and the kind of testing that catches problems before a supporter ever sees them.

I've spent much of my career supporting nonprofit and mission-driven organizations — including Mary Free Bed and The Baby Fold — so the world of fundraising communications feels like home. I love learning new platforms and finding the most efficient solution for each client and strategy.
